MindScape

Rules

  • A team will consist of 5 members.
  • The same 5 members must participate in all 4 sub-activities.
  • The activity consists of 4 sub-activities.
  • Separate rules and instructions for each sub-activity will be given on the spot before that activity begins.
  • No change of team members will be allowed during the 4 sub-activities.
  • All team members are expected to actively participate in the activities.
  • The team must follow the instructions given by the organizers for each sub-activity.
  • Participants must report to the venue on time and be ready before the activity begins.
  • The decision of the organizers will be final.
  • Any form of misconduct, unfair practice, or violation of instructions may lead to disqualification of the team.
  • The overall performance across all 4 sub-activities will be considered for the final result.
