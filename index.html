TECHNOHOLICS

An Intra-College Event

What is Technoholics?

An intra-college technical and non-technical event organized by the Kle's BCA Department, Dharwad.

Why Technoholics?

A platform for students to showcase their tech skills, creativity and talent beyond the classroom.

Who can Participate?

Any BCA students Studying in any year are welcome to take part with your house team.

Why to Participate?

To enhance your technical skills & to build solutions for real-world problems.
